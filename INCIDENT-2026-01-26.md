# Incident Report: Production DNS Outage

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-01-26-001  
**Date:** January 26, 2026  
**Severity:** Critical  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Duration:** ~1 hour

---

## Executive Summary

Production website (solfunmeme.com) experienced complete outage due to missing DNS records. Root cause was incomplete DNS configuration after nameserver migration to Vercel. Issue resolved by configuring proper DNS records in Vercel dashboard.

---

## Root Cause Analysis

### Primary Cause
Missing DNS A records for root domain (`solfunmeme.com`) and www subdomain after nameserver migration to Vercel.

### Contributing Factors
1. Nameserver migration to Vercel not fully documented
2. DNS zone management transferred from Namecheap to Vercel
3. **No monitoring/alerting system to detect outage**
4. **No distributed health check system**

---

## Resolution & Next Steps

### Immediate Fix
DNS records configured in Vercel dashboard. Site operational at https://www.solfunmeme.com
